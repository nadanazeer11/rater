# Future inspos

Brainstorm of where rater could go beyond "send a review request, capture a rating." Written from the seat of an SME founder in the ME running, say, a 3-branch salon chain, a clinic, or a small restaurant group. Not a roadmap — a wishlist to mine.

Lens: most SME owners here juggle WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, a POS, walk-ins, and a part-time marketing person. Anything that consolidates surfaces or turns a guess into a number is gold.

---

## 1. WhatsApp is the product

WhatsApp isn't a "channel" in this market — it's the channel. Bigger unlock than email will ever be.

- **WhatsApp review requests** via official Business API (template messages, opt-in respected). Approval is painful but a moat once done.
- **Unified inbox** — WhatsApp + Instagram DM + Facebook + SMS + email in one thread per customer. Today the owner's phone has 400 unread chats and no idea which were paying customers.
- **Auto-replies + templated quick-sends** in Arabic and English. "Thanks, we'll be back to you in 1 hour" beats silence.
- **Team assignment** — branch manager picks up DMs for their branch only.
- **Broadcast lists with consent tracking** — Eid greetings, new menu, Ramadan timings. Legal-safe broadcasts is a real headache to DIY.

## 2. Reputation beyond Google

Google is the anchor, but ME-specific surfaces matter more than Yelp ever did.

- **Talabat / Careem / Noon / Zomato / Mrsool review monitoring + reply.** Restaurants live or die on aggregator ratings.
- **Apple Maps reviews** — iOS share in GCC is huge and rising; almost no one tracks these.
- **Instagram & TikTok mentions** — a single viral reel about a salon can 10x bookings or destroy a brand in a week.
- **Facebook recommendations** — still strong in Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon.
- **Listings sync** — one form updates hours/photos/menu across Google, Apple, Facebook, Instagram, aggregators. Special treatment for **Ramadan hours** and **Eid closures** (currently a manual nightmare per platform).

## 3. AI that earns its keep

Not "AI" sprinkled on, but specific jobs an owner doesn't have time/skill for.

- **AI-drafted review replies** matching brand tone, bilingual (AR + EN), one-click approve. Saves face for owners who aren't confident writers in either language.
- **Sentiment + topic clustering** on feedback — "32% of complaints this month mention waiting time, up from 11%." Beats reading 200 free-text answers.
- **Weekly insight digest** to WhatsApp: "Your Marina branch dropped 0.2 stars. Top driver: 'cold food'. Staff member mentioned by name in 4 reviews: Ahmed (positive)."
- **Competitor watch** — pick 3 competitors, see their review velocity, rating trend, and what their customers complain about. Owners obsessively check this manually today.
- **Voice-of-customer rollup** for monthly owner meetings — auto-generated slide-ready summary.

## 4. Light CRM (the customer database they don't have)

Most SMEs have *zero* structured customer data outside their POS. The review pipeline is already collecting names/phones/emails — sit on that.

- **Customer profile**: visit history (if POS-integrated), past ratings, past feedback, channels they reply on.
- **Segments**: "lapsed > 60 days", "rated 5★ but never returned", "high-spend silent customers."
- **Win-back campaigns** triggered by lapse.
- **Birthday / anniversary** auto-sends with discount code.
- **Referral mechanic** — "invite a friend, both get X." Trackable codes per customer.

## 5. Operational alerts owners actually want

Owners want to know *now* when something is on fire, not in a weekly report.

- **Instant push** (WhatsApp to owner, not email) on any ≤ 2★ rating, with the customer's contact and a one-tap "call them now."
- **Volume spike alerts** — "5 negative reviews in the last 2 hours, all mention 'AC broken'."
- **Staff leaderboard** — who's named in 5★ reviews, who's named in 1★. Single most-requested feature in this category from what I've seen.
- **Branch comparison** for multi-branch owners — side-by-side rating, review velocity, complaint themes.

## 6. Surveys beyond a star

Once the review-request rails exist, the same rails do more.

- **NPS** and **CSAT** modes alongside stars.
- **Custom multi-question surveys** with branching (e.g. clinic post-visit, salon post-service, gym monthly).
- **Vertical templates** — restaurant / salon / clinic / gym / auto-service / real-estate brokerage, each with a tuned lifecycle (restaurant = same-day; salon = before next appt; clinic = 24h post-visit).
- **Photo / UGC capture** at the end of a positive flow — "share a photo of your visit, we'll feature you." Auto-suggests posting to Google Business Profile photos.

## 7. Touchpoints the owner already has

Reviews shouldn't only come from a list upload. Meet customers where the interaction happens.

- **Print-on-receipt QR / SMS request** via POS integration (Foodics, Marn, Rewaa — the big three in GCC).
- **Tablet / kiosk** mode at the counter or table.
- **NFC tags** for counters, tables, branded keychains given to clinic patients.
- **"Pay by link" + review combo** — invoice paid → automatic review request in the next message.

## 8. ME-specific table stakes

The market punishes you if these aren't right.

- **Arabic-first option**, RTL throughout (not an afterthought retrofit).
- **Hijri calendar awareness** for scheduling (Ramadan, Eid, National Days per country).
- **Ramadan mode** — auto-pause sends during iftar window, shift to post-taraweeh, surface adjusted business hours.
- **Country-aware weekends** — Fri/Sat (KSA/UAE/most GCC) vs Sat/Sun (Egypt, Lebanon, Jordan).
- **PDPL (KSA) / UAE PDPL / DIFC** consent capture and DNC list out of the box — being able to say "compliant" wins enterprise pilots.
- **Local payments** if billing in-region (Mada, KNET, Benefit, Fawry).
- **VAT-compliant invoicing** for subscription receipts.

## 9. Onboarding wins in week one

Churn happens in the first 14 days. Owners need a visible win fast.

- **POS / aggregator customer import** (Foodics, Marn, Rewaa, Talabat partner panel) so the customer list isn't empty on day one.
- **Zapier / Make.com** for everything not natively supported.
- **"Quick wins" counter** on the dashboard — "you've earned 12 new Google reviews this month, +0.3 stars" — owners screenshot this for their group chat. Cheap viral loop.
- **Embeddable widget** ("Powered by …" live-reviews carousel) on the SME's site — modest growth loop, also boosts the SME's own SEO.

## 10. Adjacent revenue lines (later)

Once the customer DB and channel rails exist, these come almost for free.

- **Loyalty / punch-card** — digital, lives in WhatsApp, no app to install.
- **Bookings / appointments** widget for salons, clinics, services. Owns the calendar = owns the relationship.
- **Tipping** — digital tip jar tied to staff who got positive reviews, owner takes a cut transparently.
- **Insurance / financing referral** for clinics & auto-services (high-margin partner deals in this region).

---

## What I'd build first if I were the founder

Ranked by "owner will pay more tomorrow" vs build cost:

1. **WhatsApp review send** (unlocks everything else in this market — even before unified inbox).
2. **Negative-review instant WhatsApp alert to owner** (cheapest possible "wow, this product saved me" moment).
3. **Multi-platform listings sync with Ramadan/Eid hours** (a real pain, no good local solution).
4. **AI bilingual review reply drafts** (owner-confidence feature; low build cost on top of any LLM).
5. **POS integration with Foodics** (the GCC restaurant unlock; instantly differentiates from any US-first competitor).
